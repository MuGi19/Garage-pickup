Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know before you book.

Where in Bahrain do you operate?
We cover all four governorates — Capital, Muharraq, Northern and Southern — including Manama, Seef, Juffair, Riffa, Hamad Town, Saar, Budaiya, Muharraq and Amwaj Islands.
How fast can a mechanic reach me?
Average arrival is around 45 minutes for priority bookings, depending on your location and time of day. Emergency assistance is dispatched first.
Do you bring the spare parts?
Yes. Our technicians arrive fully equipped with OEM-grade parts and tools for your selected service, all covered by a workmanship warranty.
How do I pay?
You'll receive a clear, upfront quote before any work begins. Pay on completion by card, BenefitPay or cash — your choice.
